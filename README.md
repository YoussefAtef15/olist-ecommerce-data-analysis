## Important Interpretation Rules

- The dataset represents a historical period, with order purchases from 2016-09-04 to 2018-10-17.
- `price` and item sales value are transaction-value proxies, not Olist revenue.
- Historical company claims are kept separate from dataset-derived findings.
- Correlation or descriptive differences are not treated as causal evidence.
- Small sample periods are not used for unsupported monthly rankings.
